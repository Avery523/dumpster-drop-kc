How Flex Dumpsters Work

Order Small, Scale Up

Every dumpster we deliver is a full-size 20-yard container. Inside, two clearly marked fill lines show the 10-yard and 15-yard levels.

Start with a 10-yard rental. If your project generates more waste than expected, just fill higher. You'll only be charged the difference for the next tier — no need to schedule a second dumpster or guess at sizing upfront.

This saves you money on smaller jobs and protects you from overordering on bigger ones.
