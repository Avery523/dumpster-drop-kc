FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What dumpster sizes do you offer?

We offer 10-yard, 15-yard, and 20-yard roll-off dumpsters. All dumpsters use our flex system — order a 10-yard and only pay more if you fill past the marked line.

How quickly can you deliver a dumpster?

We target all deliveries within 24 hours of booking across the south Kansas City metro.

How long can I keep the dumpster?

Our standard rental is 5 or 10 days. Need it longer? Just call us and we can arrange an extension.

What areas do you serve?

We serve within 25 miles of Peculiar, MO including Belton, Raymore, Grandview, Harrisonville, Lee's Summit, Overland Park and more than 25 cities across the south KC metro.

What can I put in the dumpster?

You can dispose of household junk, furniture, appliances, construction debris, yard waste, and renovation materials. Hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, and tires are not allowed.

How does your flex dumpster system work?

Every dumpster we deliver is a 20-yard container with clearly marked 10-yard and 15-yard fill lines inside. You order the smaller size and only pay more if your project fills past the marked line. No second delivery needed.

Are there any hidden fees?

Never. The price we quote is the price you pay. We believe in honest, upfront pricing with no surprises.
